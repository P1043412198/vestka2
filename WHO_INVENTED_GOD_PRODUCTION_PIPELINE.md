## 🌌 WHO INVENTED GOD — The First Time We Looked Up — Full Production Pipeline (90s TikTok)

**Topic:** Every culture that ever lived — separated by oceans, deserts and tens of thousands of years — independently invented the same thing: gods, spirits, an unseen world, the feeling of being watched. No society has ever been found without some form of the sacred. It appears to be wired into us. We survived by seeing intention behind the dark — better to mistake the wind for a predator than the predator for the wind — so we found faces in the clouds, voices in the storm, a will behind the world. And then something heavier arrived: we learned that we would die. We may be the only animal that carries the knowledge of its own end, and a mind that knows it ends cannot bear that the dark is all there is. So it reached up. It carved the first idol from a piece of stone; it built temples before it built cities, for someone it could not see. Children do it on their own — they assume the world was made on purpose, for them. Maybe we did not discover the divine; maybe we needed it into being. Or maybe the longing itself is the evidence. Either way, the same reaching has been handed down, all the way to you — and when you look up at night and hope that someone, something, is there, that is the oldest part of you: the part that refuses the dark.
**Duration:** 90 seconds → 18 scenes × 5 sec
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, cinematic anamorphic, 1080×1920 (TikTok native)
**Voice:** Female contralto, intimate documentary, ElevenLabs v3 (dynamic flow — no static pauses)
**Visual reference:** Denis Villeneuve "Arrival" awe × Terrence Malick "Tree of Life" cosmic reverence × Stanley Kubrick "2001" star-gate awe × Werner Herzog cave firelight. Reverent, cosmic, sacred, intimate — never sectarian, never mocking.
