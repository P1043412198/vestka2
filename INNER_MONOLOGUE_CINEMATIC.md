## MODERATION-SAFE BUILD

### Triggers to avoid
- Diagnosing viewers ("if you don't have inner speech you have aphantasia / autism") — no
- "Hearing voices" framing that implies psychosis — strictly avoid
- Real-person likenesses of Hurlburt, Heavey, Fernyhough, Lupyan, Nedergaard — silhouettes only
- Religious / spiritual framing — keep strictly neurological
- "We are not the same species" / divisive framing — frame it as wonder, not threat

### Safe alternatives
- Cite peer-reviewed sources (overlay text)
- Attribute the 2024 "anendophasia" term to Nedergaard & Lupyan
- Show stylized fMRI activation patterns (abstract, not actual patient scans)
- Show silhouettes of subjects — no recognizable faces
- Visualize "inner voice" as floating sound-wave ribbons or soft projected light, not cartoon thought-bubbles
- Frame the twist as universal human wonder

### Citation standards (display as CapCut overlays):
- `HURLBURT RT (2011). INVESTIGATING PRISTINE INNER EXPERIENCE. CAMBRIDGE UP.`
- `HEAVEY CL & HURLBURT RT (2008). CONSCIOUSNESS AND COGNITION, 17(3), 798-810.`
- `NEDERGAARD JSK & LUPYAN G (2024). COGNITION, 245, 105758.`
- `ALDERSON-DAY B & FERNYHOUGH C (2015). PSYCHOLOGICAL BULLETIN, 141(5).`
- `LOEVENBRUCK H ET AL. (2018). CORTEX, 103, 53-69.`

### Tone guardrails
- Never diagnostic. Never "you have a disorder."
- Tone is intimate-confessional — the voice speaks *to* you, *about* you, with restraint
- The twist is wonder, not horror
- Never claim either group is smarter or more emotional — just different modes

## ENG SCRIPT

### Voice design
Female contralto, late 30s, hushed-intimate confessional — as if telling you a secret at 2 a.m. Pacing ~145 WPM with longer pauses for existential beats. Voice feels like it's speaking directly into your inner ear — slight breathiness, intimate proximity. On the meta-twist (S17) the voice becomes almost a whisper. Most ASMR-adjacent script in the series.

### ElevenLabs v3 settings
- **Model:** `eleven_v3`
- **Voice:** female contralto (series-consistent)
- **Stability:** 35
- **Style:** 40
- **Speed:** 1.0
- **Speaker boost:** ON

### Script (with v3 tags)

```
[whispered, intimate] Some people [pause] have never heard a voice in their head.

[soft] They might be reading this right now.

[narrator] Most of us assume thinking sounds like talking. [pause] A voice that narrates.

[soft] But thirty to fifty percent of people [pause] don't experience that voice. [whispered] At all.

[narrator] In 2024, scientists gave it a name. [pause] [soft] Anendophasia. [whispered] The absence of inner speech.

[narrator] For decades, a psychologist named Russell Hurlburt asked random people, at random times, [pause] what they were thinking.

[soft] They wore beepers. When it beeped, [pause] they froze the moment, and described it.

[narrator] Some described words. [pause] Some described images. [pause] Some described pure sensation.

[whispered, dramatic] And some described — nothing. [pause] No words. No pictures. [soft] Just thinking, [whispered] without language.

[pause]

[soft] You can't see who has it. [pause] There is no outward sign.

[narrator] On fMRI, the brains look different. [pause] Inner speakers light up Broca's area. [whispered] Quiet thinkers — don't.

[soft] It changes how you remember. [pause] How you decide. [whispered] How you suffer.

[narrator] People who have it [pause] can't imagine living without it. [soft] People without it [pause] can't imagine why anyone would need it.

[whispered] Each side has been certain [pause] everyone thinks like they do. [soft] They were both wrong.

[narrator, slow] When you read this sentence — [pause] do you hear it? [pause] Or do you just know what it says?

[soft] This is the question that breaks the experiment. [whispered] The moment you ask someone —

[whispered, intimate] — they hear a voice in their head. [pause] [breath] Yours.

[soft, almost imperceptible] You might have just done it [pause] to someone.
```

**Word count:** ~228 words -> ~91 sec at 145 WPM x 1.0 speed = fits 90 sec window.
